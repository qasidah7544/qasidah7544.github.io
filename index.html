🎯 What This App Does

Spotify-status-for-Telegram is a small desktop program for Windows. It looks at the song currently playing on your Spotify account and writes that song as the first line of your Telegram bio.

You open the app once, connect your Spotify account and your Telegram account, and press start. From that moment on, your bio stays in sync with your music. When you change the song, the first line of your bio changes too. When you stop listening, the line clears itself.

Everything happens inside a simple window with clear buttons and labels. You do not need to open a terminal, type commands, or edit any files. If you can install an app and click a few buttons, you can use this one.

✨ Key Features

  • Live track display — the song you are playing right now appears in your bio, including the track name and artist.
  • Automatic updates — the app checks for changes in the background and refreshes your bio for you.
  • Works with Spotify Connect — if you play music on your phone, your laptop, or a smart speaker, the status still follows you.
  • Simple desktop window — a clean, modern interface built with CustomTkinter, designed for everyday users rather than developers.
  • Keeps the rest of your bio — only the first line is used for the song. Everything you wrote below stays exactly as it is.
  • Set it and forget it — run it once, leave it running in the background, and forget about it.
  • Lightweight and private — the app talks only to Spotify and Telegram to do its job. No song history is stored anywhere else.

🔄 How It Works Behind the Scenes

You do not need to understand the technical side to use this app, but here is a plain-language explanation of what happens after you press start.

The app asks Spotify for the track you are listening to right now. Spotify replies with the song title and artist. The app then opens your Telegram profile and replaces the first line of your bio with that song information, leaving the rest of your bio untouched.

It repeats this check every short while, so your bio is never far behind your music. The window stays small and quiet, and you can minimise it while you do other things on your computer.

💡 Tips for a Smooth Experience

  • Keep the app window open, or minimised, while you want your bio to update. If you close it completely, updates stop.
  • Make sure your computer is connected to the internet. Both Spotify and Telegram need a connection to work.
  • Use the same Spotify account everywhere. If you listen on your phone with a different account, the app will not see that track.
  • Give it a few seconds after changing songs. Bio updates are not instant, and a short delay is completely normal.
  • If you ever want your bio to stop changing, press the stop button in the app or close the window.

🛠️ Common Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The app has a normal window with buttons. If you can use Spotify and Telegram, you can use this.

Will it delete the rest of my Telegram bio?

No. Only the first line is used for the song. The text below it stays as you wrote it.

Does it post songs in my chats or channels?

No. It only edits your own bio. Nothing is sent to other people.

My bio did not update. What should I check?

Confirm that a song is actually playing, that you are online, and that the app is still open and running. Pressing stop and then start again usually refreshes the connection.

Can I undo the changes?

Yes. Stop the app and edit your bio in Telegram normally. The app will not change anything until you start it again.
